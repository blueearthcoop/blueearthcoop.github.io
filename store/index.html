Blue Earth Cooperative Store

Merch, gifts, and fun small-town finds from Blue Earth Cooperative.

The store is bigger than bakery merch. This is where we can gather designs inspired by the bakery, the creamery building, Blue Earth history, and whatever fun ideas we want to turn into something wearable, giftable, or collectible. Some pieces will be cozy and nostalgic, some playful, and some rooted in the story of this old building we are working to restore.

A little bit bakery, a little bit building, a little bit Blue Earth

New items are being added in batches. If the page looks a little sparse today, check back soon. More shirts, stickers, building-inspired designs, and fun cooperative extras are on the way.

Bakery Merch

Shirts, stickers, and favorites inspired by sourdough, kringla, market mornings, and the cozy side of the bakery.

Building & History

Designs that lean into the old creamery, restoration work, and the story this building carries in Blue Earth.

Fun Extras

Whatever else belongs here, playful one-offs, seasonal ideas, small-town inside jokes, and future favorites we have not dreamed up yet.

The best merch feels like Blue Earth itself: rooted in place, a little playful, and easy to love.
