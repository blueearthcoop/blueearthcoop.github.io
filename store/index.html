Sprout's Shop

A playful corner of Blue Earth Cooperative for merch, gifts, and good little finds.

Sprout's Shop is where the bakery, the creamery, and all our favorite Blue Earth ideas can live together. This is the home for shirts, stickers, gifts, building-inspired pieces, and whatever fun little thing deserves a spot on the shelf next.

A little bit bakery, a little bit building, a little bit Sprout

We are still shaping the shop, but this is the direction: warm, playful, rooted in the old creamery, and full of things that feel like Blue Earth.

Bakery Merch

Shirts, stickers, and favorites inspired by sourdough, kringla, market mornings, and the cozy side of the bakery.

Building & History

Designs that lean into the old creamery, restoration work, and the story this building carries in Blue Earth.

Fun Extras

Whatever else belongs here, playful one-offs, seasonal ideas, small-town inside jokes, and future favorites we have not dreamed up yet.

The best shop page should feel like Blue Earth itself: rooted in place, a little playful, and easy to love.
