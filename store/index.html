Sprout's Shop

A cheerful little shop built around Sprout, good merch, and the kind of Blue Earth finds that deserve a spot in the window.

Sprout's Shop should feel like something you would actually want to walk into: a sweet little storefront sign, a friendly face up front, and shelves full of shirts, stickers, gifts, building-inspired pieces, and local inside jokes.

A little bakery, a little gift shop, a little old Blue Earth

This direction feels lighter and clearer: warm colors, a friendly sign, a little creamery history, and plenty of room for the fun stuff.

Front Window

Shirts, stickers, and favorites inspired by sourdough, kringla, market mornings, and the cozy side of the bakery.

Old Creamery Shelf

Designs that lean into the old creamery, restoration work, and the story this building still carries in Blue Earth.

Odd Little Favorites

Playful one-offs, seasonal ideas, small-town inside jokes, and future favorites that do not fit anywhere else yet.

The vibe

Friendly, small-town, lightly vintage, and clearly tied to the bakery without feeling heavy or crowded.
