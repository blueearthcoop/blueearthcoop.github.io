Cookies From Outer Space

Kringla is one of our signature bakery items: a soft, tender Norwegian cookie shaped by hand into its classic loop. It is simple, nostalgic, and perfect with coffee, tucked into a market bag, or shared around the table.

In Sprout's Adventures, Blue Earth is home base: Main Street, Giant Days, local partners, and Heather's bakery table. The Kringla-verse is where Sprout and VK travel when a new flavor needs a story.

Baked with love. Shared with our community.
