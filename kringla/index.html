Blue Earth's Heritage Cookie

Kringla is one of our signature bakery items: a soft, tender Norwegian cookie shaped by hand into its classic loop. It is simple, nostalgic, and perfect with coffee, tucked into a market bag, or shared around the table.

We bake traditional vanilla kringla along with rotating seasonal flavors. Availability changes by bake day, so check the ordering page or Facebook for the current lineup.

A soft little cookie with deep local roots, made from scratch in small batches.
